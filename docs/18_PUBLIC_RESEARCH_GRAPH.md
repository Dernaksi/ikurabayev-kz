# Public Research Graph

## Purpose

`data/public-research-graph.json` is the bounded relationship layer for the
public Evidence Spine. It records how the reviewed public subject relates to
claims and how selected claims address a small controlled set of research
topics.

The graph is intentionally compact and non-exhaustive. It is not a knowledge
base server, ontology, publication database, inference engine, RAG index, or
runtime website dependency.

## Evidence Architecture

The public research knowledge system separates seven responsibilities:

1. **Evidence:** `data/public-facts.json` stores canonical public-safe claims,
   sources, evidence status, and review dates.
2. **Presentation:** dependency-free files under `site/` remain manually
   maintained and are not generated by this graph.
3. **Release contract:** `tools/check_public_release.py` protects the bounded
   production truth, privacy, and semantic contract.
4. **Future AI:** any real assistant remains a separate architecture and may
   consume only reviewed public facts after explicit approval.
5. **Relationships:** `data/public-research-graph.json` connects the subject,
   claims, and controlled topics without duplicating claim payloads.
6. **Living Public CV:** `tools/build_public_cv.py` deterministically selects
   reviewed facts and relations for RU/EN public drafts.
7. **Block provenance:** the generated provenance manifest maps each CV block
   back to its claim and relation IDs.

Canonical facts remain in `data/public-facts.json`. A graph relation cannot
repair, override, or strengthen a claim.

## Graph Contract

Top-level fields:

- `schema_version`: relationship schema revision.
- `subject`: stable subject ID, currently
  `person.iskander_kurabayev`.
- `topics[]`: small public bilingual topic vocabulary.
- `relations[]`: evidence-bound subject-to-claim or claim-to-topic edges.

Topic fields:

- `id`: stable `topic.*` identifier.
- `kind`: always `research_topic`.
- `public`: always `true`.
- `labels.en` and `labels.ru`: controlled public labels. Kazakh labels are not
  added before the planned language review.

Relation fields:

- `id`: stable `relation.*` identifier.
- `from`: subject ID or claim ID.
- `predicate`: one of the bounded predicates below.
- `to`: claim ID or topic ID.
- `status`: inherited evidence status.
- `evidence[]`: source IDs already present in the facts registry.
- `note`: optional limitation; it must not add claim payload or resolve an
  evidence conflict.

Relations never contain copied titles, dates, roles, organizations, DOI values,
legal status, descriptions, or other canonical claim payload.

## Topic Vocabulary

The v0.2 graph uses eight selected public topics:

- insulation parameters;
- isolated-neutral and ungrounded electrical networks;
- symmetrical components;
- electrical measurement and diagnostics;
- energy audit;
- energy efficiency;
- embedded measurement systems;
- AI-assisted engineering.

A topic edge says only that the linked reviewed claim addresses that topic. It
does not state causation, novelty, effectiveness, validation, commercial
readiness, or completeness.

## Predicate Vocabulary

| Predicate | Allowed direction | Meaning boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `holds_role` | person → professional-role claim | Relationship to the bounded role claim; currentness and dates stay in that claim. |
| `authored` | person → publication claim | Reviewed authorship of a selected work; not a complete bibliography or metric claim. |
| `inventor_on` | person → patent claim | Reviewed inventor relationship; not a statement that the patent is currently in force. |
| `leads` | person → research-grant claim | Leadership exactly as supported by the claim's evidence status. |
| `holds_credential` | person → education or professional-credential claim | Bounded academic or professional qualification; no private document metadata. |
| `received_award` | person → award claim | Bounded award relationship with the claim's translation/evidence limitation. |
| `develops` | person → roadmap-project claim | Public direction in development; never a launched-product statement. |
| `addresses_topic` | claim → research topic | Selected topical association directly supported by the linked claim. |

There is deliberately no `has_profile` relation: ORCID and Scopus remain
ordinary profile claims. There are no causal links among grants, publications,
patents, and projects.

## Status Inheritance

Graph statuses reuse the Evidence Spine vocabulary:

- `verified_public`;
- `partially_verified`;
- `owner_approved`;
- `roadmap_only`.

Every relation inherits the status of its linked claim. Relation evidence must
be a non-empty subset of that claim's evidence. A `verified_public` relation
must include at least one non-owner public source. Owner approval cannot be
silently promoted to independent verification.

`roadmap_only` is a publication-stage boundary, not a low-confidence version of
a launched result. All relations from AI Energy Auditor and STM32 / measurement
lab claims therefore remain `roadmap_only` and explicitly in development.

## Anti-Inference Rules

- Authorship does not imply publication completeness, contribution order,
  citation impact, or metrics.
- An inventor relationship does not imply that a patent is active. Legal status
  remains in the canonical dated patent claim.
- A current-role edge is point-in-time and must be rechecked before later public
  updates.
- The conflicting university start dates remain unresolved. The graph records
  only role and organization and does not select either official date.
- A topic edge does not imply a validated result, causal lineage, shared method,
  product readiness, or grant output.
- Similar words in two claims are not enough to create a relationship.
- Absence from the graph is not evidence that a relationship does not exist.

## Privacy Boundary

The graph may reference only public-safe claim and source IDs. It must not
contain private contact details, addresses, civil identifiers, credential or
document identifiers, raw scans, signatures, QR/EDS content, private paths,
contracts, unpublished data, confidential project detail, coauthor lists, or
other-person metadata.

Public source pages may expose more information than this graph records. Only
the minimum reviewed relationship is retained.

## Update Workflow

1. Update or add the canonical fact and source in the Evidence Spine first.
2. Verify the public/privacy boundary and evidence status.
3. Add only the smallest useful relationship and selected topic edge.
4. Keep relation evidence within the linked claim's evidence.
5. Run the public release validator, knowledge validator, generator drift check,
   JSON checks, and bounded mutation tests.
6. Review graph, CV, and provenance diffs through an Issue and pull request.
7. Recheck fast-changing roles and patent legal status before later public-copy
   changes.

## Future AI Boundary

The graph is not loaded by the website or local concierge. It adds no network,
API, storage, analytics, AI, or backend behavior. A future evidence-grounded
assistant may use this relationship layer only after a separate architecture,
privacy review, citation contract, and deployment decision are approved.
